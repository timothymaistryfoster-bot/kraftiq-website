Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

1. Who we are

KRAFTIQ (operated by KRAFTIQ GmbH i.Gr., Greifswald, Germany) is the data controller for kraftiq.eu. See our Impressum for full legal entity and contact details.

2. What data we collect

We collect the personal data you provide directly (name, email, delivery address, payment details processed by our payment provider) when you place an order, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us. We also collect standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) via cookies and analytics tools to operate and improve the site.

3. Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Contract performance — processing orders and subscriptions.
  • Consent — marketing emails and non-essential cookies, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legitimate interest — basic site analytics and fraud prevention.

4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest or consent. Contact us at privacy@kraftiq.eu to exercise these rights, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

5. Data retention

We retain order data for as long as required by German commercial and tax law (typically 10 years for invoices), and marketing consent data until you withdraw consent.

6. Third parties

We share data only with parties necessary to fulfil your order and run the business: payment processors, shipping carriers, email service providers, and analytics/advertising platforms — each bound by their own data processing agreements with us.

This is a template policy for a demo project and must be reviewed by a qualified data-protection lawyer before real customer data is collected.
