Frequently asked questions

How much creatine is in each serving?

Each serving (2 gummies) contains 3g of Creapure® creatine monohydrate — the clinically studied daily dose behind the EFSA-approved performance claim.

Do I need to do a "loading phase"?

No. A steady 3g/day builds your muscle creatine stores to full saturation within 2–4 weeks — no need for the old high-dose loading protocols.

Is it suitable for vegans?

Yes — KRAFTIQ gummies are 100% vegan, gluten-free, and contain no added sugar.

Will it make me bloated or hold water?

Any water retention with creatine is intramuscular (inside the muscle cell) and supports performance — it is not the subcutaneous "puffy" bloating people worry about.

Can I take it if I don't lift weights?

Yes — creatine also supports high-intensity efforts like running intervals, Hyrox, cycling sprints and everyday energy demands.

Where is it made?

Formulated and batch-tested in Germany, using Creapure®, the highest-purity creatine monohydrate available, manufactured in the EU.
