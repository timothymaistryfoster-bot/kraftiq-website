# KRAFTIQ — Product Photography Shot List

Hand this brief to a photographer, or use it as a prompt brief for AI-generated product
mockups (Midjourney/DALL·E/Ideogram) until real photography is ready.

## Brand look reference
Cream background (`#F7F3EC`), high-key/bright lighting, soft natural shadows — think
Ritual/Goli product photography, not a moody gym aesthetic. Gummies should look like a
treat, not a pharmaceutical.

## Must-have shots

1. **Hero jar, dead-center, plain cream background**
   White/cream pouch or jar labeled "KRAFTIQ — 3g Creatine", front-facing, soft shadow
   beneath. This replaces the CSS-illustrated jar currently on the homepage hero.

2. **Gummies macro shot**
   5–8 individual gummies (berry-lemon amber/pink tone) scattered on a cream surface,
   shot close/top-down, shows texture and translucency — proves "tastes like a treat."

3. **In-hand / lifestyle shot**
   Hand holding 2 gummies, gym bag or water bottle softly blurred in background. Used for
   Instagram/TikTok content and the "How it works" section.

4. **Flat-lay: jar + gummies + gym kit**
   Jar, a few loose gummies, resistance band or gym gloves, arranged flat-lay on cream/
   neutral background — for the "Who it's for" and social content.

5. **Before/after shaker comparison**
   Split shot: cluttered shaker bottle + powder tub on one side, single KRAFTIQ jar on the
   other. Directly supports the "Gummies vs. Powder" comparison section.

6. **Packaging / label close-up**
   Macro of the nutrition label panel (3g creatine, ingredients) — builds the transparency
   angle used in the "Science" section.

7. **UGC-style selfie mockup**
   Person mid-workout, gummy pack visible in gym bag pocket — for testimonials/social
   proof section and ad creative.

## Technical specs
- Minimum 2000×2000px, sRGB, JPG or WebP for web (compress to <300KB per image).
- Keep a consistent light source direction (top-left, soft) across all shots for a
  cohesive homepage.
- Deliver both square (1:1, for Instagram) and 4:5 portrait (for Reels/Stories) crops.
