# KRAFTIQ — Brand & Go-To-Market Strategy

## 1. The Product
**KRAFTIQ Creatine Gummies** — creatine monohydrate (Creapure®, German-made, the purest
grade on the market) delivered as a daily gummy instead of a powder you have to mix.
Each gummy pack delivers **3g of creatine monohydrate** per serving (2 gummies), berry-lemon
flavour, no gritty texture, no shaker bottle, no chalky aftertaste.

**Core claim (EFSA-compliant, EU-legal):**
> "Creatine increases physical performance in successive bursts of short-term, high
> intensity exercise." *(Beneficial effect obtained with a daily intake of 3g of creatine.
> Approved EFSA Article 13.1 health claim — Regulation (EU) 432/2012.)*

We only use this exact approved wording for performance claims. Any cognitive/mood/mental
health framing is positioned as "many women tell us..." anecdotal/social proof, not a
regulated claim, to stay compliant.

## 2. Why Gummies (the wedge)
Creatine only works with **daily consistency** — it's a saturation supplement, not a
stimulant. The #1 reason people stop taking it is the format: mixing powder, the texture,
carrying a shaker bag to the office/gym bag, forgetting it when travelling. KRAFTIQ removes
every point of friction: pop 2 gummies, done, anywhere.

## 3. ICP & Target Market (Europe)

### Primary ICP: "The Strength-Curious Woman", 25–42, DACH + Benelux + Nordics
- Trains 2–5x/week: strength training, hybrid/functional fitness (Hyrox, CrossFit-adjacent),
  running clubs, Pilates-to-lifting converts.
- Discovered creatine through TikTok/Instagram fitness creators in 2023–2025 ("creatine
  for women" trend), but was put off by bro-branded, chalky, tub-sized powders sold to men.
- Cares about: visible strength gains, recovery, long-term bone/muscle health, and — very
  strong purchase driver — the (widely discussed, non-regulated-claim) interest in
  creatine's role in cognitive energy, mood and hormonal-cycle-related fatigue.
- Household income: mid-to-upper, willing to pay a premium for convenience, clean labels,
  and better-tasting products (comparable to how she buys protein bars, collagen,
  matcha).
- Channels: Instagram Reels, TikTok, fitness studio partnerships, micro-influencer gifting,
  Greifswald/DACH local gym & Pilates studio cross-promotion (matches your own local-SMB
  network).

### Secondary ICP: "The Busy Optimiser", 28–45, urban professionals across EU
- Gym-goers or home-gym users who value routine simplicity (same buyer psychographic as
  Huel/Ritual/AG1 customers). Wants performance + focus without adding another bottle to
  mix. Gender-neutral but skews slightly male, dual-income, no strong price sensitivity.

### Why Europe specifically
- EFSA's approved creatine claim gives a legally clean, credible performance hook that
  US brands can't use the same way — a trust asset for EU-only marketing.
- DACH has a large, underserved premium-gummy supplement gap versus the US market (Goli,
  Bloom Nutrition, Obvi have no true DACH-native competitor yet).
- Local-first launch (Greifswald → DACH gyms/studios → EU-wide paid social) matches your
  existing local-SMB marketing network and gives you real testimonials before scaling ad
  spend.

## 4. Brand Identity

- **Name:** KRAFTIQ (from German *Kraft* = strength/power, + "IQ" = smart — strength you
  take seriously, in a smarter format).
- **Tagline:** "Strength you'll actually stick to."
- **Voice:** Confident, warm, a little cheeky — never bro-science, never clinical. Speaks
  like a knowledgeable training partner, not a lab coat.
- **Visual inspiration (3 reference sites, and what we took from each):**
  1. **Goli Nutrition (goli.com)** — gummy-category leader. Took: bold benefit-first
     hero headlines ("Say Hello to..."), huge social-proof review counters, playful
     rounded product badges, risk-free-trial framing.
  2. **Ritual (ritual.com)** — premium supplement transparency leader. Took: clean
     minimal layout, pastel-on-neutral palette, ingredient-traceability panel style,
     restrained/credible tone that avoids hype.
  3. **OLIPOP (drinkolipop.com)** — modern CPG shelf-appeal. Took: high-contrast colour
     blocking, retro-pop illustration bursts, energetic photography crops used as
     background texture rather than literal product shots.
- **Palette:** Cream background `#F7F3EC`, ink text `#1B1B1B`, electric-lime accent
  `#C6F135` (energy/performance), berry accent `#E94F64` (flavour/warmth), soft lilac
  `#E7DBFF` (secondary/cognitive-benefit sections).
- **Type:** Rounded, confident grotesk (Poppins for display, Inter for body) — friendly
  but not childish.

## 5. Site Structure (what was built)
Hero → trust bar → problem/solution → how it works (3 steps) → why creatine (benefits) →
label/ingredient transparency → "who it's for" ICP section → gummies vs. powder comparison →
testimonials → pricing/subscription → FAQ → final CTA → footer with EU legal disclaimers.
